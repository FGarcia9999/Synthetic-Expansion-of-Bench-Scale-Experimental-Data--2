## New context incorporated

1. The derivation evidence remains the historical *Candida lipolytica* UCP 988 full 2^4 factorial experiment with four center points (n=20), using surface tension as the primary response.
2. The main scientific interpretation remains unchanged: relative predictive stabilization does not imply strong absolute prediction; Gaussian Copula best preserves the confirmed KH2PO4 effect under matched-effective-n ICD and correlation geometry; TVAE shows the strongest relative stabilization; TabDDPM remains a visible negative result.
3. The manuscript now explicitly separates M1 development evidence from the post-freeze *Candida mogii* and *Candida utilis* external-validation program reported in M2. Those outcomes did not modify the Core.
4. Two further routes are recorded only as **prospectively preregistered, unexecuted scenarios**:
   - EXT-C: the public *Yarrowia lipolytica* dataset from Fontes et al. (2010), with 19 experimental assays and emulsification/surface-tension responses.
   - EXT-D: the Farias et al. (2021) route, conditional on final clarification of the SAMPLE 1-5 hierarchy and triplicate experimental structure.
5. Neither EXT-C nor EXT-D is development data. Neither may be selected retrospectively according to a favorable PEERFIX outcome.
6. Terminology was harmonized toward laboratory/DoE language. Portuguese avoids literal `corridas`; reader-facing English prefers experimental assays/data and experimental validation data. `retuning`/`firewall` wording was replaced by model recalibration / post-freeze methodological separation where possible.
7. Bioprocess meaning remains before computational terminology. The manuscript explicitly frames synthetic expansion as support for exploration and prioritization of new bench evidence, never as additional fermentation or biological replication.
